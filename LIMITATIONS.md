# Limitations

## This Is Not Investment Advice

InvestiGate is a **personal research tool** built for the author's own
investment due diligence and as an applied AI engineering learning project.

- It is **not** a financial product, service, or advisory platform.
- It does **not** provide investment recommendations, buy/sell signals, or
  portfolio advice to anyone.
- Any stance language (buy / hold / avoid) produced by the system reflects
  an automated analysis for the author's personal use and should **never**
  be interpreted as advice to others.

## Known Technical Limitations

- **LLM outputs can be wrong.** Despite grounding validation, the system
  relies on language models that can hallucinate, misinterpret context, or
  produce incorrect analysis. All outputs should be independently verified.
- **PDF parsing is imperfect.** Indian annual reports contain complex table
  layouts, scanned pages, and footnote-heavy financial statements. Some
  information may be incorrectly extracted or missed entirely.
- **Data freshness varies.** Broker tokens expire nightly. Structured data
  depends on API availability. Annual reports are published annually.
- **Single-user, self-hosted.** No multi-user support, no hosted deployment,
  no authentication layer.

## Regulatory Context

SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India) regulates investment advice
in India. This project operates within the bounds of personal research and
is not registered as an investment advisor or research analyst under SEBI
regulations.
